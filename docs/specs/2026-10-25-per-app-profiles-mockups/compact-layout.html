B. Compact PC (HK 4-row style)

11 keys per row, so keys about 35% wider than the full layout. Keeps Ctrl, Tab, Fn and arrows; digits and symbols on long-press or Fn. Esc is Fn+Tab. ⌨ jumps to your other keyboard.

Today's full layout has 15 key-widths per row; A has 10, B has 11. Both need roughly the same work, except A also needs a ?123 symbol page.
